Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Pharmapath Pathology Lab ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

When you book a test, request a report or enquire about medicines, you share your name, mobile number, age, address, selected tests and preferred time. This website does not store these details on any server — the form opens WhatsApp with your message, and you choose whether to send it.

Why we use it

  • To schedule sample collection or your lab visit
  • To share your test report with you
  • To respond to medicine and home care enquiries

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Your reports

Lab reports are health data. We share them only with the patient, on the mobile number registered at booking, or with someone the patient authorises. Reports are not published on this website.

How long we keep it

We keep records as long as required by medical and legal record-keeping rules, then delete them securely.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, or withdraw consent, by messaging us on WhatsApp at +91 91728 99472 or visiting the lab.

Third parties

This site uses WhatsApp (Meta) for messaging, Google Maps for location and Google Fonts. Their own privacy policies apply when you use them.
